One suggestion I'd add that wasn't in the earlier ideas

Create a "Bookshelf of Memories" instead of a simple envelope grid. The envelopes can be grouped into subtle chapters:

🌙 For Difficult Days (lonely, overwhelmed, giving up, scared)
🏡 For Missing Home (miss home, miss me, first festival away, need a hug)
🌸 For Beautiful Days (happy, proud, achieve something, smile)
🌌 For Quiet Nights (can't sleep, angry, motivation)
✨ One Last Letter (hidden until everything else is opened)

This gives the site a sense of progression and makes it feel like she's exploring a curated collection rather than clicking isolated cards. It also naturally makes the experience feel richer and more substantial without losing the elegant minimalism that you've already achieved.
